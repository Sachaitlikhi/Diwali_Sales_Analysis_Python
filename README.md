# Diwali_Sales_Analysis_Python

## 🎯 Project Objective

The **Diwali Sales Analysis** project aims to perform an in-depth Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on real-world transactional data collected during the Diwali festival season. The goal is to identify key customer demographics and purchasing behavior trends to support data-driven business decisions.

This Python-based analysis utilizes libraries like **Pandas**, **NumPy**, **Matplotlib**, and **Seaborn** to uncover insights such as:

- Spending patterns by gender and age group
- State-wise order volume and purchase amounts
- Customer occupation influence on sales
- Most popular product categories during Diwali
- The impact of marital status on buying behavior

### 📌 Key Insight:

**Married women aged 26–35 from states like Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka, working in IT, Healthcare, and Aviation sectors, are the most active buyers**, showing a strong preference for **Food, Clothing, and Electronics** categories.
